# Craft criteria — Pixel Oak Profile

- The 3:2 Hero is the first and largest authored object at 860px and 320px.
- The full Oak, central Y negative, trunk and roots remain visible without crop.
- Forest Oak reads first; the internal facet reads second and never becomes a
  person, trophy, hook, cutout, second logo, or polar highlight.
- All cells align to integer coordinates with `shape-rendering="crispEdges"`.
- Light/dark geometry is structurally identical; only declared tokens change.
- The poster has no embedded identity copy or product claims.
- The Evidence root field is the only authored black field and stays decorative.
- Native GFM retains one H1, the 02–05 ledger spine, Q/H/E/N order, three proof
  links, three principles, one open loop and one counterexample action.
- At 320px, `scrollWidth === clientWidth`, every link is in bounds, and no
  semantic content depends on either image.
- No human recognition study is required. Review evidence consists of source
  checks, theme-structural equivalence, DOM metrics and direct artifact images.
