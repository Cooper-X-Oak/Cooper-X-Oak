## Verdict

D01 has materially improved and now reads first as a wide-canopy tree under
expert inspection. It is not accepted because the declared five-observer
recognition evidence is absent. D04 has no selected production candidate:
Balanced is closest, but its bone projection still becomes a high-priority
person/lightning-shaped form in dark mode.

The submitted 320 PNGs are invalid evidence. Each is a 320×214 crop containing
only `#F2EFE4`; the intrinsic 864px SVG was cropped from the left rather than
scaled to the complete 54×36 viewBox.

## Directed revisions

1. Close D01 recognition evidence without presenting the design name or
   context before each observer's first answer. Preserve raw answers and the
   860/minimum-size split.
2. Generate genuine light/dark 320 projections with the complete viewBox and a
   fail-closed pixel/bbox report proving background, forest, structure, signal,
   Oak bounds and input hash.
3. Use 02 Balanced as the only topology baseline. Keep at least 2u forest
   between structure and the open Y, enter the trunk after the Y closes, limit
   trunk structure to 2u while retaining at least 4u forest trunk, and return
   displaced area to a shallow left-shoulder inset so total structure remains
   78–116u².

Revision-003 remains a draft route. The UI-mutation barrier stays closed and no
concept in revision-002 may enter the production allowlist.
