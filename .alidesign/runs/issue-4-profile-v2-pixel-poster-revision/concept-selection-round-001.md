# Component-domain concept selection — round 001

Status: `needs_revision`

Controller: `design-controller-019f664b-38d1-79f3-ba6b-2a9c059f5c6c`

This is a reversible `component_domain` concept decision, not an evaluation
round and not a new workflow state. The UI-mutation barrier remains closed.

## Evidence reviewed

- `concepts/01-shoulder-weighted-light.png` plus dark projection
- `concepts/02-balanced-light.png` plus dark projection
- `concepts/03-grounded-light.png` plus dark projection
- `concepts/manifest.json`

All mechanical connectivity, hole, and area checks passed. They did not prove
visual recognition or professional craft.

## Verdict

Reject all three. Do not select the least-bad variant.

The shared D01 silhouette reads first as a mushroom, lamp, face, hair mass, or
game sprite. The canopy is a thick horizontal cap, the dominant negative is a
face-like horizontal slot, the trunk lacks a continuous load-bearing body, and
the roots read as legs or a platform. D04 then amplifies the error: its long
high-contrast vertical mass becomes a body/mask and competes with the Oak.

The failure belongs first to
`visual_design.oak_monument_skeleton`, then to
`visual_design.oak_structure_plane_topology`. D02 composition, D03 color,
D05 Evidence, D06 page grammar, and the selected IA remain in force.

## Directed revisions

1. Redraw D01 so the monochrome forest silhouette independently shows a
   connected central crown, left/right shoulders, an open Y whose negative is
   not a horizontal slot, a continuous thick trunk, and unequal but balanced
   roots with no platform or separated feet.
2. Rebuild D04 on the accepted silhouette as a narrow left-side inset surface.
   It may connect crown, left branch, trunk side, and one root exit, but it must
   not occupy the Y, create a second body, or become the dominant light shape in
   dark mode.
3. Make the three variants visibly different across at least two functional
   zones. Unlabelled observers must distinguish top-heavy, balanced, and
   grounded mass without reading the manifest.

## Re-submission contract

Keep the rejected concept files unchanged. Submit a new append-only concept
directory with monochrome silhouette, three 860px light/dark pairs, three
320px projections, mechanical manifest and pairwise differences. Re-submit to
the same controller before `semantics_ready`.
