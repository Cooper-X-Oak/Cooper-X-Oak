## Current experiment

- Subject: **Capability Routing**.
- Public scale: a bounded capability-routing experiment, not a finished AI
  governance product or universal human-control layer.


none may appear as an unsupported manifesto.

## Selected art direction

Selected direction: **Pixel Laboratory**.

The selection supersedes the earlier recommendation of `Proof as Identity` but
does not delete the three original hypotheses. Those files remain the reversible
comparison that led to this choice.

Locked visual authority:

- Pixel language is a **complete visual grammar**, not a small corner accent.
- Its aesthetic parent is **editorial design**, not retro computing or an indie
  game interface.
- Professional trust must come from clear judgment and evidence; form cannot
  substitute for content.
- Use a single-column editorial long page.
- Use modern sans-serif typography for reading; pixel geometry is reserved for
  the identity glyph, numbering, markers, and short labels.
- Use a white-led field with near-black type and restrained solid black areas.
- Recalibrate the existing bright green into a **deep forest green**.
- Rebuild the avatar as an **abstract Oak glyph**. It may evolve substantially
  while retaining the Cooper Oak, pixel, and green lineage.
- The first impression must be authored rather than templated.

Prohibited readings and devices:

- game HUD, inventory, sprite sheet, mascot scene, or arcade styling;
- terminal chrome, prompt window, scan line, CRT, or hacker-console styling;
- dashboard grid, telemetry, fake controls, progress bars, self-issued badges,
  or product-status theater;
- cards as the default container, gradients, shadows, glow, animation, remote
  assets, or decorative AI imagery;
- pixel fonts for sentences or body copy.
