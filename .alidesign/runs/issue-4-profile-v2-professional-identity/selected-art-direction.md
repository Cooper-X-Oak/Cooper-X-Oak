# Selected art direction — Pixel Laboratory

Status: selected direction for reversible concept exploration.
Authority: `decision-contract.md` and explicit user approval.
Production status: not a visual-design specification; not UI-mutation authority.

## Thesis

Pixel geometry becomes Cooper Oak's complete identity grammar while evidence,
plain language, and editorial hierarchy remain the source of professional trust.

The direction should feel authored at first sight without becoming a game brand,
terminal theme, or synthetic AI interface.

## Signature

Create one abstract Oak glyph from a strict pixel unit and a small set of optical
rules. The glyph may significantly evolve from the current avatar, but it must
remain traceable to three inherited facts:

1. a stepped pixel construction;
2. a green identity field;
3. an Oak reading through growth, branching, canopy, trunk, counterform, or
   rings.

The glyph is the only dominant graphic. It may organize the Hero and section
markers; it must not become a repeated icon pack.

## Visual grammar

- Canvas: white-led, with restrained solid black fields.
- Identity color: deep forest green; exact token is a concept decision, not yet
  a production token.
- Type: modern sans-serif for name, lead, and body; short technical labels may
  use a quiet monospace fallback, but never a decorative pixel font.
- Grid: one editorial reading spine with a visible pixel unit and intentional
  alignment changes between desktop and narrow composition.
- Surface: flat. No shadow, gradient, glass, glow, bevel, texture, or simulated
  depth.
- Grouping: use whitespace, rules, baseline, scale, and contrast. Do not wrap
  the four experiment fields in cards.

## Content grammar

```text
COOPER OAK
Experiments in keeping humans in control of AI-assisted work.

CURRENT EXPERIMENT / CAPABILITY ROUTING
QUESTION → HYPOTHESIS → EVIDENCE → NEXT

WORKING PRINCIPLES
Make control explicit.
Route work to specialists.
Claims need evidence.

OPEN LOOP
writing-loop-harness
```

The three concept boards use the same real copy so the review compares design
systems rather than different marketing claims.

## Internal variants required

The concept round must compare:

1. **Branch Register** — the Oak glyph grows from a disciplined branching grid.
2. **Counterform Canopy** — the Oak is cut from one strong field and uses
   negative space as the primary signature.
3. **Ring Ledger** — stepped growth rings bind identity, history, and the
   evidence ledger.

Each board must include glyph, Hero, and Current Experiment sample. A chosen
variant may later combine one secondary property from another board only through
an explicit decision; it may not collapse the three boards into an average.

## Failure gates

Reject a board when any of these is true:

- it could become another developer's identity by swapping name and color;
- the Oak reads primarily as a game sprite, environmental logo, terminal icon,
  or temporary app mark;
- pixel geometry reduces headline or experiment readability;
- green, rules, labels, or modules create a fake control surface;
- evidence copy becomes decoration or is replaced by status theater;
- the narrow composition is merely a scaled desktop board.

## Boundary to production

Selecting Pixel Laboratory resolves the high-level art direction only. Product
UI mutation remains blocked until one internal variant is selected and separate
`visual_design`, `craft`, implementation, evidence, and independent final-review
capabilities are resolved and completed.
