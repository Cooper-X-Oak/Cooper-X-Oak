## Proof ordering guidance

The first AI-native proof should be the strongest artifact for the chosen
visitor and conversion. Under current evidence, `goal-to-do` is the safest
lead because it is released and installable. `writing-loop-harness` or
`loop-preflight` may become the lead if the user chooses a more systems-oriented
story and accepts explicit experimental/prototype labels.

`LongYinMod_RisingFame` and `valvetrade-pipeline` should not compete as equal
interest categories with the AI direction. They prove that Cooper's release,
support, boundary, and test discipline exists outside Agent language.

## Copy review rule

For every future headline, summary, visual label, alt text, and project entry:

1. identify the row that authorizes the claim;
2. retain its maturity language;
3. link or immediately expose the direct proof;
4. remove any metric or credential not present here;
5. append a route/evidence revision if the underlying proof changes.
